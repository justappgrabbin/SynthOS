1. PHILOSOPHICAL ALIGNMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Both systems share a fundamental insight: MEANING IS STRUCTURE.

Foundry (2024) posits that the 5-base dimensional pattern (WHERE/WHAT/WHEN/WHY/WHO) 
recurs across all scales of reality. The W asks; the H holds. The question contains 
the structure of its answer.

Klein (1973) demonstrated that narrative meaning emerges from a semantic deep structure 
network of objects and relations (O-R-O triples), where the network itself IS the 
meaning, and surface language is merely a projection.

THE SYNTHESIS:
The W-H pairs ARE the semantic triples. Each question-answer pair is a directed 
edge in the semantic network. The 5 dimensions are the 5 fundamental relation types 
that structure all possible meaning.
